identity(X) is never held twice
dated(X) is never held twice
knows(X, Y)  ⇒  identity(X)
knows(X, Y)  ⇒  identity(Y)
coin(X, Y)  ⇒  identity(X)
coin(X, Y)  ⇒  identity(Y)
date(X, t)  ⇒  identity(X)
date(X, t)  ⇒  dated(X)
bond(X, Y, t)  ⇒  identity(X)
bond(X, Y, t)  ⇒  identity(Y)
bond(X, Y, t)  ⇒  dated(Y)
dated(X)  ⇒  identity(X)
coin(X, Y) and X ≠ Y  ⇒  knows(X, Y)
bond(X, Y, t) and X ≠ Y  ⇒  knows(X, Y)
