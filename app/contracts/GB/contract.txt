GB: Grassroots Bonds
Alice, Bob and Carol are persons.
1. ■ Minting.  Alice may decide to issue Alice-coins, and Alice-bonds of a maturity later than their own date.
2. ○ Date.  Alice keeps their date at the current date.
3. ■ Swap.  Alice and Bob, who know each other, may jointly decide to swap coins and bonds they hold.
4. ■ Payment.  Alice may decide to pay Bob-coins they hold to Bob, whom they know.
5. ■ Redemption.  Alice may decide to redeem a Bob-coin, or a matured Bob-bond, they hold against any coin or bond held by Bob, whom they know.
6. ○ Pricing.  Alice prices their offerings in Alice-coins and accepts them in payment at those prices.
7. ■ Escrow.  Alice may deposit coins and bonds with Carol for Bob, on a condition the three agree; Carol and Bob may jointly decide to release them to Bob when the condition is met, and Carol and Alice may jointly decide to return them to Alice when it fails; each act is between two parties who know each other.
Pragmas
Coins: the coins Alice holds.
Bonds: the bonds Alice holds.
Minting: Alice [Mint coin] <amount> [Mint bond] <maturity> <amount>
Swap: Alice [Swap] <issuer given> <maturity given> <amount given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted> <amount wanted>
  Bob: Alice asks to swap coins or bonds with you.
Payment: Alice [Pay] <amount>
Redemption: Alice [Redeem] <maturity given> <issuer wanted> <maturity wanted>
Escrow: Alice [Deposit] <issuer> <maturity> <amount>
  Carol: Alice asks to deposit coins or bonds with you.
  Carol [Release] <issuer> <maturity> <amount>
  Bob: Carol asks to release coins or bonds to you.
  Carol [Return] <issuer> <maturity> <amount>
  Alice: Carol asks to return coins or bonds to you.
